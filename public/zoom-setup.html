Connect Zoom through 2ndBrain.

Your original 2ndBrain login stays in place. Zoom is a separate connected account used by marketplace tools.

  1. Open 2ndBrain Marketplace or Settings → Integrations.
  2. Select Connect Zoom and approve access through the Supabase Zoom provider.
  3. Install and launch Meeting Minutes from My Workflows.
  4. Use Manage Zoom in 2ndBrain to reconnect or disconnect. Changes apply without restarting Meeting Minutes.

If setup is required

A 2ndBrain administrator needs to configure the shared Supabase Zoom provider, dedicated connection callback, read scopes and central token storage. Users do not enter Zoom Client Secrets in Meeting Minutes.

Your meeting data

Zoom tokens stay encrypted on 2ndBrain’s server. Meeting Minutes uses an authenticated read API. Saved transcripts and analyses remain in this browser’s Saved library.
